THE DATA BEHIND IT

Source-linked, not black-box.

ScoutForge uses a published subset of the European Commission's ESCO occupation–skill classification to suggest terms and adjacent roles. The search comparison tests only taxonomy records, never people. Your role text stays in this browser session.
